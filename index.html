About

Background

"It works" is the floor, not the ceiling.

Software engineer based in San Diego, CA, focused on backend and full-stack systems — the parts of an application that have to keep working when traffic spikes, inputs are malformed, or a third-party API goes down. I work mostly in Python and TypeScript.

I think depth-first. I care about why something works, how it breaks under load, and how to make it genuinely better. That carries into the unglamorous parts of the job — input validation, failure handling, compliance, the edge case nobody filed a ticket for. Usually that's what separates a demo from something people can rely on.

I came to engineering by an indirect route. I ran an e-commerce business for three years — sourcing, inventory, fulfillment, and customer support — and closed it in 2023 to study computer science full-time at UCSD. Before that I spent a year tutoring Java, C++, and data structures, which is where I learned that if you can't explain a design decision simply, you probably don't understand it yet.
