About

Background

Software engineer and founder based in San Diego, CA. I build things that work — full-stack platforms in Python and TypeScript, agentic AI systems using local LLMs and open-source tools, and now a company of my own. My edge isn't just the stack I know — it's how fast I pick up the ones I don't.

I think depth-first. "It works" is the floor, not the ceiling. I care about why it works, how it breaks under load, and how to make it genuinely better. That mindset carries into everything I ship — and everything I build a business around.

Currently founder of AtoZFlow, a platform helping small businesses increase profitability by automating repetitive daily tasks and staying reachable to their customers 24/7. Built for complexity — from distributed systems to real-time data pipelines. Drawn to hard problems worth solving.
